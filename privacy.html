Privacy Policy

How this demo website handles the information you submit.

Overview

This is a demonstration website. The privacy policy below describes how the demo application handles submitted information and is provided as a template for a production deployment.

Information We Collect

When you submit a form on this site — a newsletter signup, a valuation request or a contact message — we store the details you provide (name, email, phone, address and message) in the application’s own database so our team can respond to your inquiry. We do not collect analytics or advertising data in this demo.

Favorites

Properties you save as favorites are stored locally in your own browser and are never transmitted to our servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use submitted information solely to respond to your inquiry (demo). We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

Your Choices

You may request correction or deletion of any information you submitted by contacting us through the contact form.
